Football • Offensive Line (O-Line)

Positions: Tackle & Center

I play football, holding down the offensive line as both tackle and center. The O-line is the engine of the entire offense. At center, I have to make the pre-snap calls, read defensive fronts, and deliver a clean, accurate snap before engaging my block. At tackle, I protect the edge against outside pass rushers and kick out to open up explosive running lanes for our backs. It takes raw power, quick feet, smart leverage, and total trust in your brothers on the line.
